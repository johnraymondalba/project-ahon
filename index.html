AHON

Only takes One Hop to Help

An offline-first mesh network for flood-prone Nabua and Naga City — citizens broadcast SOS, status, and needs; LGU responders triage and dispatch, even with zero signal.

Citizen Broadcast your status and needs, see nearby responders, or volunteer to help others — even with zero signal. LGU / Command Center Monitor every incoming report, let AI triage rank who needs help first, and dispatch teams with confidence.

Prototype for BETT Show Asia · mesh network, map data & AI triage are simulated for demo purposes · see README.md
