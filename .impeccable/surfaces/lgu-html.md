## Direction contract

THESIS: The LGU Command Center is the live plotting chart itself, full-bleed on the desktop; the priority list and AI Triage are drawers that slide over it, never boxed dashboard widgets sitting beside a map.

OWN-WORLD: Inherits the Typhoon Track Chart world set on index.html, in its light paper-chart register for sustained daytime desk work — cream chart-paper ground (#F2EFE6), navy ink (#0A1628), grey-blue graticule (#2A3B52), amber relay-trace (#F5B942), red reserved only for SOS/critical (#E8452C), green for cleared (#4A9B6E); Overpass display, system-ui body, Overpass Mono for every coordinate/timestamp/count readout.
